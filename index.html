FAQ

Questions, answered

Do I need any equipment?

No. Every workout uses your body weight: squats, push-ups, glute bridges, lunges, planks, wall sits and mobility. A bit of floor and a wall is enough.

How does Restfit adapt to my sleep?

Each morning you answer a quick check-in: when you went to bed, when you woke and how you feel. Under 6 hours of sleep or feeling a bit tired makes today an easy day, sore legs keep leg work out, and unwell makes it a rest day.

Is it good for beginners?

Yes. Restfit starts easy and adds one rep or one set at a time, only after sessions that felt right.

Does the camera record me?

No. The optional camera rep counter uses a pose model that runs on your phone. Frames are processed and dropped at once, never saved or uploaded. You can tap to count instead.

Is the coach a chatbot?

The coach answers from Restfit's coaching rules, on your phone and without internet. The "AI" in Restfit is the on-device pose model and the plan that adapts to you every morning.

Do I need an account?

No. There is no sign-in. Your plan, check-ins and workouts stay on your phone.

Is Restfit free?

Yes, with no ads: 3 workouts a week, the camera for 3 moves and 3 coach questions a week. Pro adds a daily adaptive plan, the camera for every move, your full history and home-screen widgets, with a 7-day free trial.

Is this medical advice?

No. Restfit is for general fitness and wellbeing. If pain is sharp or lasting, please see a doctor.
